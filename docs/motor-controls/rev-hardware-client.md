# Rev Hardware Client 2
Rev Hardware Client 2 is the software we use to control Rev related hardware.

To connect to it, use a usb and connect directly to the SparkMAX, depending on how its wired, 1 device may show up or all supported device will.

This will mean that reconfiguring IDs and names will take longer.
